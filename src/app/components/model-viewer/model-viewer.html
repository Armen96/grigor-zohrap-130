House over storage, 10.8 → 10.0 × 14.7 m

Concept model, Revision 07 draft: 14.70 m deep so the footprint (152.9 m²) stays under 40% of the 385.05 m² plot. Bedrooms face the street; living and kitchen face the garden and open onto a back terrace. The stair from the garage ends at its own door. Side walls are blind (neighbours).

How the plan is read
  • Plot: left boundary straight, right boundary angles inward (10.80 m at the street, 10.00 m at the rear wall). Both side walls are party walls on the boundary.
  • Footprint: 152.9 m² = 39.7% of 385.05 m², under the 40% limit. Revision 05 was 17 m deep (176.8 m², 45.9%).
  • Ground: storage (≈ 91 m² clear) with its own 1.35 × 2.0 m shower room (WC, basin, shower) in the rear-left corner of the garage, door from the rear storage; a private garage 5.1 × 8.5 m clear (widening to 5.56 m at the street) for two cars side by side behind a 4.8 m garage door with a pass door. Behind the cars the garage stays empty, with a 2.4 m wide space between the WC and the L-stair (up the right wall, turns left). A door at the right end of the ground-floor rear wall leads from the rear storage to the garden, sheltered by the garden-stair landing above it.
  • Street side upstairs: a 16.0 m² master bedroom (4.0 × 4.0) with its own 2.85 × 1.8 m en-suite shower room behind it, an 11.5 m² work room (2.88 × 4.0) in the middle, and on the right a second bedroom that is one open 2.7–3.0 × 5.8 m room (≈ 16.5 m²): the sleeping area at the street window and an open dressing area at the back by the stair wall, with no wall or door between them. Behind them the hall is one straight 4.0 × 1.65 m strip: the master door near the corridor, the work-room door and the bedroom door together at its far end, the bedroom door opening between the sleeping area and the dressing area. All three rooms have street windows.
  • Arrival: the stair ends at its own door; the stair stays closed off from the house. The door opens onto a 2.20 m corridor between hall and living.
  • Service shaft: 0.4 × 1.0 m, between the en-suite and the main bath on the corridor side, from the upper floor to a vented cap on the roof. It vents both baths and carries cables, including the roof solar panel cables, with an access panel from the corridor. Below the upper floor there is no shaft: a vent duct and a cable tray run under the storage ceiling, the duct to the storage shower room and the tray through the wall to the electrical panel / solar inverter in the garage. A second, smaller shaft (0.4 × 0.5 m) runs from the roof down to the upper floor only, in the corner of the stair enclosure beside the stair door, with an access panel facing the landing.
  • Middle: hall (1.65 m deep), main bath (2.85 × 2.45, tub with shower, vanity, WC, washing machine) off the corridor, and the corridor. The baths and the corridor run back to the living wall.
  • Garden side: a 5.2 × 3.4 m living room with a glazed door and a window onto the 10.1 × 2 m back terrace, plus a solid stretch of wall for the air conditioner (outdoor unit on the terrace), and a separate 4.25–4.4 × 3.4 m kitchen/dining behind its own wall and door, with a 3.35 m window onto the terrace. Both windows start 0.4 m above the floor. Their front wall is one straight line, set 0.2 m into the space behind the stair, which is fixed by the ground floor. The provisional garden stair starts from the terrace. A simple vertical steel ladder on the rear wall, at the right end of the terrace, goes up to the roof.
  • Two roof lights, over the stair and the corridor, bring daylight into the middle of the house, since the side walls cannot have windows. Solar panels on the roof (18 panels, shown for demonstration) bring their cables down the service shaft.
  • The model assumes 3.5 m floor-to-floor below and 2.9 m clear upstairs; the plan sheet leaves final rise and headroom to coordinated design. Furniture and finishes are illustrative.
