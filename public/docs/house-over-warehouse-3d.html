House over storage, 10.8 → 10.0 × 14.7 m

Concept model, Revision 06: 14.70 m deep so the footprint (152.9 m²) stays under 40% of the 385.05 m² plot. Living and kitchen face the street, bedrooms face the garden; the stair from the garage ends at its own door. Side walls are blind (neighbours).

How I read the plan
  • Plot: left boundary straight, right boundary angles inward (10.80 m at the street, 10.00 m at the rear wall). Both side walls are party walls on the boundary.
  • Footprint: 152.9 m² = 39.7% of 385.05 m², under the 40% limit. Revision 05 was 17 m deep (176.8 m², 45.9%).
  • Ground: storage (≈ 91 m² clear) with its own 1.35 × 1.55 m WC in the rear-left corner of the garage, door from the rear storage; a private garage 5.1 × 8.5 m clear (widening to 5.56 m at the street) for two cars side by side behind a 4.8 m garage door with a pass door. Behind the cars the garage stays empty, with a 2.4 m wide space between the WC and the L-stair (up the right wall, turns left).
  • Street side upstairs: living room and kitchen/dining across the full width, both opening onto the 10.8 × 2 m front terrace.
  • Arrival: the stair ends at its own door; the stair stays closed off from the house. The door opens onto a 2.35 m corridor between living and hall.
  • Middle: bath (2.7 × 3.8, tub, shower, double vanity, washing machine) against the bedroom wall, and the corridor. The living room is 0.3 m deeper than the kitchen.
  • Garden side: hall, two 4.05 × 3.7 m bedrooms and a 1.0–1.2 m passage to a provisional garden stair.
  • Roof lights over the stair, bath, hall and dining area bring daylight into the middle of the house, since the side walls cannot have windows.
  • Floor-to-floor 3.5 m below, 2.9 m clear upstairs. Furniture and finishes are illustrative.
