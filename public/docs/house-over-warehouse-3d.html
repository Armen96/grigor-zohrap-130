House over warehouse, 10.8 → 10.0 × 17 m

Concept model, Revision 05: real plot width, 10.80 m at the street narrowing to 10.00 m at the rear wall. White plaster with oak cladding, flat roof with parapet, warm wood interior. Side walls are blind (neighbours).

How I read the plan
  • Plot: left boundary straight, right boundary angles inward (10.80 m at the street, 10.00 m where the building ends). Both side walls are party walls on the boundary.
  • Ground: L-shaped rental warehouse (≈ 100 m² clear) with WC; private garage front right (5.55–5.90 × 6.80 m); U-stair behind the garage, reached only from the garage.
  • Upper: front terrace across the full width (10.8 × 2 m) plus a 4.45 × 4.3 m side terrace; enclosed lobby above the garage (≈ 6.2 × 4.3 m gross), landing into the house.
  • House: living + kitchen/dining strip (4.0 m wide) on the left, bath (3.7 × 1.5) and laundry behind the stair, hall, two 3.9 × 3.7 m bedrooms at the rear, 1.3–1.5 m passage to a provisional garden stair.
  • Bath, laundry and kitchen get daylight from roof skylights, since side windows aren't possible.
  • Floor-to-floor 3.5 m below, 2.9 m clear upstairs. Furniture and finishes are illustrative.
