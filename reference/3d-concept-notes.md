# House over warehouse, 9 × 17 m

Concept model built from Revision 04. White plaster with oak cladding, flat roof with parapet, warm wood interior. Side walls are blind (neighbours).

How I read the plan

- Ground: L-shaped rental warehouse with WC; private garage front right; U-stair behind the garage, reached only from the garage.
- Upper: front terrace 9 × 2 m plus a 4 × 4.3 m side terrace; enclosed lobby (5 × 4.3 m) above the garage, landing into the house.
- House: living + kitchen/dining strip (3.4 m wide) on the left, bath and laundry behind the stair, hall, two bedrooms at the rear, 1.2 m passage to a provisional garden stair.
- Bath, laundry and kitchen get daylight from roof skylights, since side windows aren't possible.
- Floor-to-floor 3.5 m below, 2.9 m clear upstairs. Furniture and finishes are illustrative.
